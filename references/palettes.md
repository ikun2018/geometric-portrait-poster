# Palette presets

Use these as controlled starting points, not as an instruction to put every color in every portrait.

## 色卡 01 — 紫蓝霓虹（如图）

Black background; deep violet and indigo hair/shadows; cobalt and cyan clothing; teal midtones; selective acid yellow, coral red, hot pink, and white highlights. Use a bold white outer contour. Make the face the brightest, most chromatically varied region.

## 色卡 02 — 复古暖色

Deep espresso background; burgundy, terracotta, burnt orange, mustard, dusty pink, cream, and muted turquoise accents. Use an off-white outer contour only if it helps separation. Keep the result like a print poster rather than neon.

## 色卡 03 — 青橙电影感

Charcoal background; navy and deep teal shadows; cyan and muted sky-blue midtones; amber and orange highlights; small ivory accents. Use high contrast at the face, but keep the clothing calmer.

## 色卡 04 — 黑白编辑感

Black, charcoal, slate, cool grey, white, with one small accent color chosen by the user or defaulting to crimson. Use strong negative space and a restrained, magazine-cover mood.

## 色卡 05 — 原图提取

Derive 5–8 dominant colors from the supplied image, then add near-black for structural shadows and an optional white contour. Preserve the source photo's overall temperature; do not introduce unrelated neon accents.
